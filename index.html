06 · Questions

Questions.

Who is it for?

Solo AI-influencer creators and small studios running one or more synthetic characters — anyone who needs the same face to survive a whole feed, not one lucky frame.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes. The product’s public demo assembles prompts with no key and no account — the live exhibit on this page mirrors its logic. The full studio is the licensed part.

Do I need API keys?

Building prompts from a scene description needs no key at all. Reference analysis and the AI features — scene enhancer, critic, assistant — use your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini or OpenRouter key; OpenRouter offers free models. Image and video generation always happens in your own engine accounts.

What happens when engines change?

The adapters absorb it. Engine facts live in swappable adapter layers, and the canon keeps growing — your passport and your techniques carry over.

Does it generate images and videos?

No — by design. Influencer OS builds the prompts; you paste them into Nano Banana Pro, Kling, Seedance, Veo or Omni Flash with your own accounts. The studio never touches your generations and takes no cut.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser — localStorage and IndexedDB. Characters, history and keys stay on your machine; nothing is synced to us. Three things do cross the network: the one-time license check, the optional AI features if you turn them on (they call your chosen provider directly with your own key), and anonymous production analytics. Backup export and import are built in.

The face drifts between frames — what do I do?

Fill the passport completely, keep Anomaly Lock on, and don’t mix worlds in one feed — the studio flags a mixed-world feed. Add at least two realism markers per scene.

The picture looks “neural” — how do I fix it?

Keep the realism panel on, listen to the cliché guard, don’t remove the planned “glitch” — unrealistic perfection can reduce visual quality. Realism is added positively: ask for real skin, pores and sensor noise instead of banning things.
