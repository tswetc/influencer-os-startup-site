4 ACTS · DEMO DATA

ACT 1

The character

The passport records the intended appearance, reference angles and distinctive details to include in production instructions. This is a creative specification, not a guarantee about the rendered output.

Identity block — demo character June (public demo data).

ACT 2

The prompts

The same passport can inform prompts for multiple engines, each with its own formatting rules. External output must be checked separately.

EXHIBIT A · ASSEMBLED PROMPT · 148 WORDS · DEMO DATAREAL OUTPUT
IDENTITY: young woman in her early twenties, soft oval face, wide-set grey-green eyes, dark blonde hair with grown-out roots tucked behind one ear, light natural brows, faint smile lines, small silver stud earrings, relaxed unhurried presence. SCENE/POSE: bedroom floor by the window, hard morning sunlight stripes, sitting cross-legged, looking at the light. CLOTHING: oversized worn t-shirt. ANOMALY ANCHOR: small chip on the corner of the left front tooth, visible when she smiles. Faint diagonal scar through the tail of the right eyebrow. Single dimple on the left cheek only. Three small moles in a line on the right side of the neck. Do not normalize or correct these features. TECHNICAL: black iPhone 15 Pro Max in a transparent silicone case, shot on iPhone, Smart HDR, computational photography look, slightly oversharpened, casual framing, imperfect framing, no beauty filter, unretouched, natural skin texture with visible pores and fine vellus hair.

ACT 3

The outputs

Four illustrative visual groupings: character references, lighting, wardrobe and beauty. Captions describe the images; no unverified generation engine or accuracy score is attributed to them.

Showcase lighting studies from the existing public product gallery.

Gallery images are presented as visual studies. Without controlled source-to-output evaluation, they should not be treated as verified same-identity render sequences.

ACT 4

What carried

The character passport and distinctive-detail fields remain available when prompts are reassembled. The visual outcome still depends on the external model and reference quality.

EXHIBIT B · WHAT CARRIED · THE ANOMALY BLOCKDEMO DATA
TOOTH CHIP → "small chip on the corner of the left front tooth…" EYEBROW SCAR → "faint diagonal scar through the tail of the right eyebrow…" LEFT DIMPLE → "single dimple on the left cheek only…" NECK MOLES → "three small moles in a line on the right side of the neck…" This block rides in every prompt, for every engine. It is never summarized away. Do not normalize or correct these features.
