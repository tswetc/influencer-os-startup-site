01 · Modules

What the studio actually does.

01

The passport

Six blocks of identity canon: appearance, anomaly lock, per-mode adherence, device register, reference photos, vision summary. The demo character June shows the public version. Optional canon — her places, objects, habits, voice — bends the packs toward her.

02

Adapters

Each engine wants its own syntax and length. Adapters keep one framework and re-target it per engine — photo, motion, B-roll, dialogue, refinement. Scenes may be written in Russian or English; the studio parses them into fields and always assembles the prompt in English.

03

The taste layer

Worlds A/B/C set the register: Diary is honest and digital, Raw strips all glamour, Staged is the only world where film vocabulary is allowed. On top sit 24 technique atoms — each one a reason for the frame, light, state, surface and artifact — 18 scene packs with 92 prompt-ready scenes, and 4 output formats.

04

The five engines

Verified pipeline: Photo → Nano Banana Pro · Animate → Kling · B-roll → Seedance · Dialogue → Veo · Refine → Omni Flash. Kling multishot packs 2–6 numbered shots into one prompt with a 15-second budget.

05

Formats & the feed

Frame · Series (4–8) · Shoot (6–12: heroes, details, one cutaway) · Feed (12–30). A feed is planned, not generated: hero → hero → B-roll → hero → detail, one deliberate imperfection, captions in her voice, her city’s season baked into the light. Cutaway frames never carry identity — the canon tests it.

06

Quality gates

Prompt Doctor, cliché guard, realism panels, a hit journal with per-engine stats — and a self-check suite of 160+ assertions that verifies the canon on every build.

07

Export

Shot lists with day markers and an AI-disclosure checklist, bundle export as JSON / TXT, a Series Card as PNG.

08

License & data

One-time license, server-side Gumroad key check, offline cache. Characters, history and keys stay in your browser. What crosses the network: the one-time license check, your own-key AI calls if you enable them, and anonymous production analytics.
